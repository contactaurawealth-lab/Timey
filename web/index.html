4 Science-Backed Study Engines

Different cognitive tasks require different intervals. Timey adapts to your natural flow state.

🍅

Classic Pomodoro (25/5)

The golden standard. 25 minutes of laser-focused work paired with 5-minute recovery intervals and an automatic 15-minute long break every 4 cycles.

🧠

Ultradian Deep Work (50/10)

Engineered for complex academic subjects, coding, and mathematical proofs that need 15+ minutes just to reach peak flow. Full 10-minute cognitive reset.

🌊

Flowmodoro (Dynamic Ratio)

Never get interrupted by an alarm when deep in thought. Count up unconstrained. When your flow naturally pauses, your break is automatically calculated (1:5 ratio).

📝

Strict Exam Simulator

Simulate real examination conditions. Disables pause, rings halfway and 5-minute warnings, and penalizes leaving the app with distraction flags.

Engineered to Never Freeze or Drift

Android battery managers aggressively kill timers. Timey solves this with a dual hardware bridge.

🔔

Android 14 Foreground Service

Persistent notification with live countdown and interactive [Pause], [Resume], and [+5m] actions directly from your lockscreen.

⏰

Hardware RTC Wakeup

Uses AlarmManager.setAlarmClock() which wakes the CPU at the exact target millisecond, bypassing Doze mode and OEM battery optimizers.

🛡️

Strict Anti-Distraction

Leaving the app during focus blocks triggers an urgent 10-second grace countdown before recording an abandoned distraction mark.
