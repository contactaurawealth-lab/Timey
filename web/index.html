5 Science-Backed Focus Engines

Different cognitive tasks demand different work rhythms. Timey provides precision engines for every study style.

🐉

135:15 Deep Work

Engineered for intense technical deep work. 135 minutes of uninterrupted focus followed by a 15-minute recovery interval.

🍅

Classic Pomodoro (25/5)

The golden standard. 25 minutes of focus with 5-minute recovery sprints and automated 15-minute deep recovery every 4 cycles.

🧠

Ultradian Sprint (50/10)

Matches natural human 90-minute ultradian rhythm cycles for computer science, engineering, and writing blocks.

🌊

Flowmodoro

Unconstrained count-up flow. When your focus session naturally pauses, your recovery break is calculated using an automated 1:5 ratio.

📝

Exam Simulator & Open Focus

Strict continuous exam hall countdown with midway warnings, no pause allowance, and open-ended stopwatch focus.

Engineered to Never Die & Never Mute

Android battery killers and Doze mode ruin normal timers. Timey guarantees rock-solid reliability.

🔊

Guaranteed Audible Ringing

AlarmRingingService fires on the USAGE_ALARM audio stream with full-screen heads-up display and continuous vibration, overriding notification silence.

⏰

Hardware RTC Wakeup

Uses AlarmManager.setAlarmClock() which wakes the CPU at the exact target millisecond, completely bypassing Doze mode and OEM battery optimizers.

🛡️

Focus Shield & App Blocker

AccessibilityService intercept immediately halts attempts to launch distracting apps (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, etc.) with PIN lock security.

🎧

7 Procedural Soundscapes

Zero-asset offline PCM audio: Rain, Forest Birds, 10Hz Binaural Alpha Waves, Brown Noise, White Noise, Campfire, and personal MP3 playlist manager.

📊

Room Database Analytics

Persistent local database tracks focus sessions, study streaks, 7-day Canvas weekly charts, and subject breakdown without external cloud tracking.

🧘

Fullscreen Immersive Mode

Hides Android system bars with WindowInsetsControllerCompat. Minimalist typography, ambient rotating quotes, and accidental termination protection.
