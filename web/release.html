🚀 What's New in v1.1.0

⏱️

135:15 Deep Work Study Cycle

Engineered for extended technical study, programming, and mathematical research. 135 minutes of immersion followed by a 15-minute recovery interval, with phase-skipping and custom interval sliders.

🛡️

Focus Shield & App Blocker

Native AccessibilityService intercept that detects window transitions and displays an inspiring blocker overlay with topic countdown. Supports PIN lock to prevent bypassing and preset distraction lists.

🔊

Guaranteed Alarm & Background Resilience

New AlarmRingingService plays on USAGE_ALARM stream, overriding notification silence. Service runs as START_STICKY with 8-hour WakeLock and RTC hardware alarm wakeups.

🧘

Fullscreen Immersive Focus

Dedicated distraction-free view utilizing WindowInsetsControllerCompat to hide Android navigation and status bars. Features clean circular ring, ambient quote cycler, and PIN protected exit.

🎧

7 Procedural Soundscapes & MP3 Player

Zero-asset, 100% offline PCM audio generation: Rain & Thunder, Forest Birds, 10Hz Binaural Alpha Waves, Brown Noise, White Noise, Campfire, and Lo-Fi Chill Pulse. Also supports personal MP3 playlist import.

📊

Room Database Analytics

Persistent SQLite logging of every focus session. Includes calendar-accurate streak flames, custom Canvas 7-day weekly bar chart, and subject-wise study time breakdown (Math, CS, Physics, etc.).
